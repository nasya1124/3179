Where the forests grow

Forest area varies across Australia

Queensland has the largest reported native forest area. Its 2023 total is 51,750 thousand hectares. The Northern Territory, Western Australia, and New South Wales follow. The remaining jurisdictions report smaller areas. These figures show total area, not forest share. The map locates totals; bars support closer comparisons.

Forest area: ABARES, Indicator 1.1a.i (2023) . Map boundaries: ABS, ASGS Edition 4 digital boundary files (2026) .
