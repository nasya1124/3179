FOREST COMPOSITION

What they are made of

Australia’s native forests include many tree types and species. Eucalypt forest dominates reported area, while smaller forest types occupy distinct environments. Herbarium records show where three selected native tree species were collected. These records describe documented specimens, not tree abundance.

FIRE AND FORESTS

Fire in the forests

Fire shapes Australian forests differently across regions. Planned burns support land management. Unplanned fires vary more between years. The 2019–20 fire year reached a national peak. These charts compare annual forest area burnt. The heatmap breaks that area down by state.

Data: ABARES, Indicator 3.1b (2024), Figure 3.1b-2 and Table 3.1b-5 . Areas are reported in thousand hectares by Australian financial year. Each year covers July to June. CC BY 4.0.
